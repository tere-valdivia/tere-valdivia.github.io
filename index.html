About Me

I am a chilean Radioastronomer currently working at the ESO Headquarters in Munich, Germany. My research is focused on how protostars and their disks gain their mass. My expertise is in the submillimeter regime, in particular, the kinematic analysis of molecular gas to understand the flow of material at diverse physical scales.

Education: PhD (2024) at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität and Max Planck Institute for Extraterrestrial Physics. BSc and MSc from Universidad de Chile

Research Interests: Early stages of star and planet formation, radioastronomy, interferometry, and astrochemistry.

Research

My research so far has focused on the early stages of star and planet formation, and how the environments shapes this process. My strong suit is the analysis of molecular lines, through which I study the kinematics of gas that surrounds protostars.

My recent efforts have been focused on characterizing streamers. I am keen to determine what is their role in the star and planet formation process. Nevertheless, I have been involved in projects covering a broad range of star formation topics, from the survival of molecular clumps under extreme irradiation, to the rich chemistry found in outflows powered by young protostars. Collaborations:

Protostars and Disks, Global Evolution (PRODIGE) NOEMA Large Program:
I am an active member of the PRODIGE Max Planck/IRAM Large Program (PI: Caselli and Henning), where I have been involved in the data reduction and imaging team, and published two first-author papers so far: see here and here. For more information about PRODIGE, see the program website.

Quantifying the connection from the envelope to the disk with MIOP-PRODIGE (ANTIHEROES) 30m Large Program:
I am an active member of the ANTIHEROES IRAM 30m large program (PI: Gieser), where I have been involved in the telescope observations and data analysis. Our first paper highlights the need for the 30m to recover the correct spectral shape of molecular lines in the envelopes of protostars.

Probing the Physics of Star formation (ProPStar):
I am an active member of the ProPStar collaboration, consisting of NOEMA and 30m observations of the southern region of the Perseus Molecular Cloud, and in the near future, covering several more regions in Perseus with the VLA. So far, we have published three papers, one of which I authored.
